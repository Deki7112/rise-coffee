PASTE THIS AT THE BOTTOM OF layout.css
=======================================

Experiment 3, Week 9.

Open layout.css, click at the end of the very last line, press Enter
a couple of times, and paste everything below the equals signs.

TWO blocks. md is the tablet, sm is the phone. The ranges do not
overlap, so no width ever matches both.

Both properties on every line. flex-basis is what makes a column take
a whole line. max-width on its own will not stack anything, because
.col already says flex-grow: 1 and flex-basis: 0%.

=======================================

/* ============================================
   TABLET   481 to 768
   ============================================ */

@media screen and (max-width: 768px) and (min-width: 481px) {

	.col-md-1  { flex-basis: 8.33%;   max-width: 8.33%; }
	.col-md-2  { flex-basis: 16.67%;  max-width: 16.67%; }
